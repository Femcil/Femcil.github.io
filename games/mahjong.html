Mahjong

Tiles 144 Moves 0 Time 0:00 Pairs open 0
Wall 0
How to play. 136 tiles, no flowers, no kongs. Each turn you draw a tile, then discard one. Complete hand: four sets (triplets or runs) plus a pair, or seven pairs. Anyone can take a discard to make a pong (triplet) or finish their hand; only the player after the discarder can take a chow (run). Claimed sets are shown face-up. Winner scores: complete hand 1, self-draw +1, concealed +1, dragon or seat-wind triplet +1 each, all triplets +2, seven pairs +3, mixed one suit +3, pure one suit +6, all honors +8. Self-draw: every opponent pays that total. Win on a discard: the discarder pays it for everyone. The dealer keeps the deal after winning or a draw.
