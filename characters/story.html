The Full Story

Warning, it's prone to changes randomly.

It starts with the Light versus The Decay in a battle for the one true god. Admist the fight one great hit between them both at the same time caused a big bang making our world. Over time the universe cooled and became vast and distant.
After the Protogen Lore by Malicerisu plays out in this universe almost similarly but resulting in the destruction of their society, many Millennias passed without any other intelligent life. Later on, as the universe continues to expand, the Trappist system forms.
The star too hot to form any life especially with planets not really too distant from each other it would be super long until life forms, as the planets themselves cooled, a dead, shutdown, drifting, ancient protogen space craft crashes onto the surface of Trappist 1d. The star goes supernova and burns into a red dwarf. Now with the star now much cooler and planets close, life can begin. From the explosion, trappist 1b and 1c lost their atmosphere but 1d thickened.
The first intelligent life to be formed on 1d, looked simular to Earth's humans and built a great empire on the middle of continent 1, with most of the villages/tribes concentrated on a great mountain far from the shores but protected from the wilderness; their farming sometimes poor unless for rice. As many more nations form and the species starts to modernize, a great discovery was made. Ancient tech, from many lightyears away was discovered on Continent 1, and the humans decide to DNA swab and replicate everything. Their code was unrecoverable, but was rebuilt for human purposes anyways under a secret government mission for biological AI.
The lab made many prototype species to target what can exactly replicate protogen biology and out off all the species on the planet, cats fit best. When the first few prototypes were being experimented on, Teskasu and Javaberus were born. They were immedially seperated on unnatural birth, grew shockingly quickly, and Javaberus was modified with a exposed ear chip. Showing modifying biology with technology was a success and it was time to make the first real Protogens. (the humans found the name from the ancient ship wreckage)

Character

Heila

Warning, it's prone to changes randomly.

Heila was a lab experiment for the first official Protogen species on Trappist. She was paired with her sister Midnight in creation, and both were given special runes. Heila was given a star, and works best during the day when it's bright. Midnight was given a moon. After Heila, Midnight, Teskasu, and Javaberus got kidnapped by a mysterous dark web organization that had the intention to sell them for the highest bitter, they were able to be free when the boat got shot down at Protogenian shores. Heila worked in Midnight's cafe called the Protogen Cafe.
